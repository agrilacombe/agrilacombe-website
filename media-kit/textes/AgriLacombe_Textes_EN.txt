LONG DESCRIPTION
----------------
Based in Saint-Mathias-sur-Richelieu, in Quebec’s Montérégie region, Ferme Agri Lacombe is a family farm. We grow Spanish onions, red onions, garlic, asparagus and pumpkins, which we harvest and store on the farm. We also offer camping firewood from April to November. New crops join the line-up every season.
