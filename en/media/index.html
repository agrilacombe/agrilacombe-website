Short description

Ferme Agri Lacombe is a local, family-run producer in Saint-Mathias-sur-Richelieu, in Quebec’s Montérégie region. We grow a range of fresh vegetables through the seasons: Spanish onions, red onions, Music garlic, garlic scapes, pumpkins, asparagus… with more to come!

Long description

Based in Saint-Mathias-sur-Richelieu, in Quebec’s Montérégie region, Ferme Agri Lacombe is a family farm. We grow Spanish onions, red onions, Music garlic and its scapes, asparagus and pumpkins, which we harvest and store on the farm. We also offer camping firewood from April to November. New crops join the line-up every season.

Why Agri Lacombe

Fresh local vegetables, grown for you.

Agri Lacombe is a family farm in Saint-Mathias-sur-Richelieu, in Quebec’s Montérégie region, about 40 km from Montreal. We grow onions, garlic, asparagus and pumpkins.
