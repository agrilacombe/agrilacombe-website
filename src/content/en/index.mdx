## Welcome to Agri Lacombe

Nestled in the heart of the fertile Montérégie region, near the charming town of Saint-Mathias-sur-Richelieu, Quebec, Agri Lacombe is a welcoming family farm. It's the culmination of a dream born from a deep passion for the land, now cultivated with care by Bruno and Mérédith Lacombe.

We grow premium **asparagus**, sweet **Spanish onions**, crisp **red onions**, and local **pumpkins**, and we also offer quality **camping firewood**. We focus on sustainable practices that respect our environment and ensure the highest quality products reach you. Our commitment is to work with passion and foster a connection between the land, our family, and yours.

Explore our website to learn more about our story, our dedication to sustainability, the products we grow and offer, and where you can find them.
